01 / About

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in Biostatistics at Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health, where I develop rigorous and scalable methodologies in causal inference, statistical learning, and trustworthy AI.

Current appointmentDepartment of Biostatistics
Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health

MentorProfessor Rui Duan

Doctoral adviserProfessor John D. Storey

My work is motivated by questions in genetics, genomics, and the integrative analysis of multimodal biomedical and environmental data. I am especially interested in methods that remain reliable under complex dependence, population heterogeneity, and real-world data constraints.

I received my PhD in Quantitative and Computational Biology with a Graduate Certificate in Statistics and Machine Learning from Princeton University.
